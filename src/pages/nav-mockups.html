How operators/managers jump between Operador, Tablero, Gráficas, Admin. Each mockup wires the pattern on the operator screen at 390 px. Pick one (or mix) and I apply to real code.

01 · BOTTOM TAB BAR

iOS-style tab bar

Fixed bottom · 4 icons always visible.

02 · HAMBURGER DRAWER

Trade: hidden by default · one extra tap.
